My Story

"Why does it feel so confusing to use something that's supposed to make life easier?"

This curiosity led me to pursue a Master's in Interaction Design from the University of Queensland, where I deepened my understanding of human-computer interaction, user research, and prototyping.

I discovered that UX isn't just about making things look nice—it's about solving real problems for real people.

Today, I love transforming complex problems into simple, user-friendly solutions and continually learning how design can make technology more human.
