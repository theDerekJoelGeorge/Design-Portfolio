SeaSaw - Exploring Collaboration and Non-Verbal Communication Through Physical Balance

2025

University of Queensland | Interaction Designer

Sea Saw was developed as part of the course DECO7385: Physical Computing Studio at the University of Queensland. The project set out to explore how balance can be leveraged as a medium for non-verbal communication and collaboration. Designed as an interactive installation, Sea Saw allows two participants to stand on individual balance boards and jointly control a shared surfboard in a virtual ocean.

My role in the project involved researching precedents, designing the interaction flow, building the balance board prototype, and integrating sensor data with Unity to create a responsive gameplay environment.

Challenge Research Problem Ideation Solution Testing Reflection
THE CHALLENGE

What were we looking to solve?

Collaboration is not always verbal. Subtle forms of communication like posture, gestures, and body movement, often play an equally important role in coordinating with others. Our challenge was to design a system that fosters teamwork through embodied interaction rather than speech.

"Sea Saw reimagines balance as a collaborative input, encouraging participants to rely on timing, rhythm, and physical awareness to achieve a shared goal: collecting coins together."
THE RESEARCH

What inspired the design of Sea Saw?

We conducted extensive user research through interviews, surveys, and observational studies to understand the learning behaviors and preferences of students. Our research revealed key insights about engagement patterns, learning preferences, and barriers to active participation.

"I learn better when I can interact with the content and collaborate with my peers."

The Problem

Traditional learning platforms lack interactivity and fail to engage students effectively, leading to poor learning outcomes and decreased motivation.

IDEATION

How did we approach the solution?

We brainstormed multiple concepts focusing on interactivity, collaboration, and gamification. Through iterative design and user feedback, we refined our ideas into a comprehensive learning platform that combines interactive content, peer collaboration, and engaging visual design.

The Solution

SeaSaw is an interactive learning platform that combines engaging content, collaborative features, and personalized learning paths to create an immersive educational experience.

USER TESTING

Did the solution meet user expectations?

We conducted usability testing with students to evaluate the platform's effectiveness. The results showed significant improvements in engagement, learning outcomes, and user satisfaction compared to traditional learning methods.

REFLECTION

What did I learn from this project?

This project taught me the importance of user-centered design in educational technology. I learned how to balance functionality with engagement, and how to create solutions that work for diverse learning styles. The experience also strengthened my skills in collaborative design and iterative prototyping.
